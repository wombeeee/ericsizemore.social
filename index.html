About

Always testing ideas — for thirteen years that's mostly meant K12 ed tech. Came up as an IT director (school networks, Chromebooks, the printer wars), now spend my days at CDW training educators and admins on Google Workspace, ChromeOS, and Gemini. 200+ sessions in. I teach from the console, not the bullet point.

Beyond work, “testing ideas” is just being curious out loud. National parks with my wife Makayla and our dogs Cooper and Riley. WWII reading. Theology arguments. Sooners 🏈, Thunder 🏀 — record optional.

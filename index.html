About

I'm Eric — Senior Education Consultant at CDW, where I've spent 13 years in K12 ed tech, first as an IT director keeping school networks (and Chromebooks) alive, now training educators and admins on Google Workspace, ChromeOS, and Gemini. 200+ sessions in. I teach from the console, not the bullet point. Outside of work, I chase national parks with my wife Makayla and our dogs, Cooper and Riley. Genuinely curious — happy to talk WWII history or theology with anyone who'll listen. Sooners 🏈, Thunder 🏀, and firmly convinced printers were sent here to test us.

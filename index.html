About

Always testing ideas — for thirteen years that's mostly meant K12 ed tech. Came up as an IT director (school networks, Chromebooks, the printer wars), now lead an onboarding & enablement team at CDW — still teaching from the console, not the bullet point, 200+ sessions in.

Lately the ideas under test are about AI: what it actually changes in schools, in knowledge work, and in how a team runs. The writing and experiments here are where I work that out in public.

Beyond work, “testing ideas” is just being curious out loud. National parks with my wife Makayla and our dogs Cooper and Riley. WWII reading. Theology arguments. Sooners 🏈, Thunder 🏀 — record optional.
